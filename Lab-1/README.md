## Lab 1 – Requirements Engineering

### Project: Personalized Meal & Diet Subscription Manager

The Personalized Meal & Diet Subscription Manager is a healthcare and telemedicine system designed to manage personalized weekly meal plans and meal deliveries.

The system generates meal plans based on a subscriber's:
- Metabolic constraints
- Calorie goals
- Allergen exclusion list

It also allows subscribers to:
- View their personalized weekly meal plans
- Modify upcoming meal deliveries by pausing, skipping, or rescheduling

Dietitians can update subscriber dietary constraints, while the system tracks the fulfillment status of meal deliveries.
